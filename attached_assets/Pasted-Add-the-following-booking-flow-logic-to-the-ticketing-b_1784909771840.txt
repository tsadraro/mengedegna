Add the following booking-flow logic to the ticketing/booking app in this
project. Check whether a booking flow (operator selection, trip search,
checkout) already exists before building new components — extend what's
there rather than duplicating it.

1. OPERATOR LIST ORDERING
   Wherever transport operators/carriers are listed for the customer to
   choose from (e.g. a dropdown or list of bus/transport companies), sort
   them alphabetically by name. This should be the default sort everywhere
   that list appears, not just on one screen.

2. SEAT SELECTION BEFORE PAYMENT
   After the customer picks a trip/operator and before they reach the
   payment screen, show them a seat map or list of currently available
   seats for that specific trip and require them to select one. The
   customer must not be able to proceed to payment without a seat
   selection. Once a seat is selected, hold/reserve it for that booking
   attempt so two customers can't both pay for the same seat.

3. DELIVERY METHOD CHOICE BEFORE PAYMENT
   Also before payment, ask the customer how they want to receive their
   ticket once it's confirmed. Two options:
     a) Boarding-pass format — a formatted digital ticket similar to an
        airline boarding pass (passenger name, trip/operator, date, time,
        seat number, a QR/barcode, booking reference), viewable/downloadable
        in-app or emailed as an image/PDF.
     b) Text message — a concise SMS containing the essential details
        (operator, date, time, seat number, booking reference).
   Store this choice with the booking so it's available at the delivery
   step.

4. CORRECT ORDER OF OPERATIONS
   The flow must be, in this order: browse operators (alphabetical) ->
   pick trip -> select seat -> choose delivery method -> enter payment ->
   process payment. Do not let the customer skip seat selection or
   delivery-method choice to reach payment directly.

5. TICKET DELIVERY ONLY AFTER PAYMENT IS VERIFIED
   Do not generate or send the ticket at the moment payment is submitted.
   Wait for confirmation from the payment provider/gateway that the
   payment actually succeeded (not just that it was submitted) before:
     - Finalizing the seat reservation as sold (not just held).
     - Generating and sending the ticket in the format the customer chose
       in step 3 (boarding pass or SMS).
   If payment fails or is not confirmed, release the held seat back to
   available inventory and do not send a ticket.

6. EDGE CASES TO HANDLE
   - Seat becomes unavailable between selection and payment (e.g. someone
     else bought it first) — tell the customer and let them pick another
     seat before payment, don't charge them for an unavailable seat.
   - Customer abandons checkout after selecting a seat — release the held
     seat after a reasonable timeout (e.g. 10-15 minutes) so it doesn't
     stay artificially unavailable forever.
